## D1. When the hotel places the event order — RESOLVED by the stand

**Source:** [STAND] Valio Aimo staff member at the Junction stand, Sat 14 Nov 2026, relayed by a team member. Name not yet recorded.

**What they said (paraphrase):**
1. The event order is normally placed **months in advance**.
2. There is then usually **one clarification call, about 2–3 weeks before** the event.
3. What gets clarified, most often: **head count** (they know about no-shows by then) and **items and item counts** in the order.
4. Less often: **changed allergen information**.
5. Occasionally: a **special-order item**. [TEAM] potentially worth leaving out of scope.

**Ruling change:** D1 was SCOPE-SPLIT / UNRESOLVED on the ordering moment. Now: **the order exists months ahead and is amended once, ~2–3 weeks out.** None of the three agents' patterns (late single order; two-step; folded into regular deliveries) was right.

**Corroboration:** the 2–3 week call lines up with the Finnish hotels' own specification deadlines (7–21 days by group size at VALO Hotel & Work; 2 weeks at S Group and Rantapuisto), which is when the hotel itself learns final numbers and diets. [INFERRED]

**Knock-on effects on other disputes [INFERRED, to confirm]:**
- **D3 (event context never reaches the wholesaler): OVERTURNED in part.** The call is hotel ↔ Valio Aimo sales rep, and head count and allergen changes come up on it, so the wholesaler **does** receive event context, through a person by phone. No public **system** channel carries it (still CONFIRMED). The context lives with the rep, not in the order. [STAND + INFERRED]
- **D6 (lead times):** less critical for the main order, since it is placed months ahead. Still relevant for the amendment.
- **Agent C's C49** ("event orders are long-lived and edited, not one request-response") is supported.
- **D2 (1,000 L):** unaffected on volume; the timing makes the brief's "by Monday" look like a late amendment or a non-event order, not the normal event path.

**Answered (team, 14 Nov):** the clarification call is between **the hotel and its Valio Aimo sales rep**.

**Open points on this answer:**
- Who initiates the call, the hotel or the rep? And who at the hotel takes it: chef, F&B manager or purchaser?
- Was she describing hotels specifically, or event and catering customers in general?
- "Months in advance": how many, roughly?
- Is the months-ahead order a real order in the webshop or SAP with a delivery date, or a reservation or forecast held by a sales rep?

## D2. Is 1,000 L of lactose-free milk plausible? — DECIDED by the team

**Facts (unchanged from D-review):** 4–10× one event's need at 0.1–0.25 L per guest; no public rule blocks it as a webshop order; the mock can't fill it from one product with a real stock figure. After D1, the timing ("by Monday") doesn't match the event path either.

**Decision:** [TEAM, 14 Nov] **The 1,000 L is the brief's illustrative number.** The journey map follows the event journey (order months ahead, amended ~2–3 weeks out). It does not treat 1,000 L as a realistic event volume, and it does not map a separate routine-restock journey.
