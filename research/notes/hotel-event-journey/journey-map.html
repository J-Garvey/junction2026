How a Finnish hotel gets the food for a large event

From the booking months out to service on the night: who decides what, which documents move, and what Valio Aimo actually sees. Built from three research reports, a cross-examining review, and answers from Valio Aimo staff at the stand.

Timing

The event order goes to Valio Aimo months in advance, then gets amended on one clarification call about 2–3 weeks out. Stand

What changes

Mostly head count (no-shows are known by then) and items and quantities. Sometimes allergen information. Occasionally a special-order item. Stand

Where the event lives

No order field carries head count, menu or event type. Valio Aimo learns the event context only through its sales rep, by phone. Sourced Stand

Why 2–3 weeks

It matches the hotels' own deadline for the client's final numbers and diets: 7–21 days by group size, or 2 weeks. Sourced Inferred link

Team decisions

  • Team The brief's 1,000 L of lactose-free milk is an illustrative number. At 0.1–0.25 L per guest, one event of 1,000 guests needs about 100–250 L, so this map follows the event journey and does not treat 1,000 L as an event volume.D2
  • Team Special-order items are possibly out of scope. They come up only occasionally on the clarification call.D1

Settled along the way

  • The US and Irish 72-hour guarantee does not describe Finland. Finnish hotels use a specification deadline (7–21 days or 2 weeks) plus a short window for last changes (3 working days where stated).D1, D9
  • Valio Aimo suggests substitutes in the webshop at order time and the customer chooses. Kespro, for comparison, phones before delivery.D5
  • An agent misread the S Group cancellation table. Its percentages are how much can be cancelled free, not how much is charged.D10
