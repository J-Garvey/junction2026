# Spike orders under time pressure: event caterers, contract caterers' event arms, stadium/arena/festival food operations

Scope note: off-premise/event catering and big live events only (hotels and conference venues are covered by another researcher). Research date: November 2026. The research used about 20 search/fetch calls, so coverage is broad but shallow in places. Two primary PDFs (the Flow Festival 2024 food press release and a Fazer Slush 2017 release) could not be text-extracted, so their contents are only known from search snippets. Geography is labelled [FI], [UK], [US], etc.

---

## 1. Segments: who buys for spikes, and how big the spikes are

### Takeaway
In Finland, the large spike buyers are a few hospitality groups running arenas and stadiums (NoHo Partners at Nokia Arena, Restel's Food & Events at Veikkaus Arena and Helsinki Ice Hall events, Fazer/Compass at the Olympic Stadium). Below them is a long tail of independent restaurants that trade at festivals, plus contract caterers' event teams (for example Fazer at Slush). In the US, Levy plays the same role at a much larger scale. Each event is a one-off peak on top of an otherwise predictable base.

### Cited Findings
- [FI] Nokia Arena (Tampere): NoHo Partners Oyj is the catering partner and "provides offerings for all Nokia Arena events". NoHo Meetings & Events runs facilities and catering for events of every size. — [Nokia Arena – Organize an event](https://nokiaarena.fi/en/organize-an-event/); [Nokia Arena Food and Drink](https://nokiaarena.fi/?p=5635)
- [FI] At the 2023 Ice Hockey World Championships in Tampere, Nokia Arena had 22 restaurants and bars plus 63 suites with food and drink ordering. "Close to two thousand VIP guests" were served per game, with 12,000 spectators in the hall. One outlet (Hook Wings) sold 10,000 wings per day during the previous year's tournament (2022). — [Kespro article, 29.3.2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla)
- [FI] Veikkaus Arena (Helsinki): Restel Oy's "Food & Events Tapahtumaravintolat" built a concert-specific F&B range for Abreu's 20th-anniversary concerts (March 2026), with the artist and Warner Music Live. Kiosk, corridor and suite (aitio) selections can now be tailored to each event type, and products are developed in Food & Events' own kitchens. — [Restel/STT press release, 17.3.2026](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/71882959/abreun-konsertit-saavat-oman-ravintolatarjontansa-veikkaus-arenalla?publisherId=55320854)
- [FI] Food & Events previously created an event-specific, fully plant-based menu for Sideways Festival at Helsingin Jäähalli (Helsinki Ice Hall), and says it operates at "many event arenas and cultural centres". — [Restel/STT, 17.3.2026](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/71882959/abreun-konsertit-saavat-oman-ravintolatarjontansa-veikkaus-arenalla?publisherId=55320854)
- [FI] Helsinki Olympic Stadium: Fazer Food Services was selected (announced 15.2.2019) to provide restaurant and catering services at the stadium and tower, covering meetings, events, hospitality and "major events and international events". It was a public procurement under the Finnish Act on Public Procurement and Concession Contracts, starting in spring 2018. The criteria were concept, resources, performance, quality management, responsiveness, price/quality ratio and innovation. — [Fazer press release, 2019](https://news.cision.com/fazer-group/r/fazer-food-services-selected-as-the-food-service-partner-of-the-helsinki-olympic-stadium,c2742454)
- [FI] Compass Group Finland now lists Bistro Stadion at the Olympic Stadium as one of its restaurants. — [Compass Group FI – Bistro Stadion](https://www.compass-group.fi/ravintolat-ja-ruokalistat/muut-avoimet-ravintolat/kaupungit/helsinki/olympiastadion/bistro-stadion/)
- [FI] The Olympic Stadium hosted five major concerts in 2025, produced by Live Nation Finland, Warner Music Live and All Things Live. — [City of Helsinki news, 2025](https://www.hel.fi/en/news/high-praise-for-concerts-at-olympic-stadium-in-2025)
- [FI] Slush 2017 (Messukeskus, 30.11–1.12.2017): Fazer Food Services was responsible for the food. About 10,000 visitors attended per day, served by a team of about 100 Fazer food professionals. (Taken from the search snippet; the PDF could not be parsed.) — [Fazer/Cision release, 2017](https://mb.cision.com/Main/964/2403901/760447.pdf)
- [FI] Slush 2022 had about 12,000 attendees and used Selfly Store smart cabinets as an additional food sales channel. — [Selfly Store – Food service at Slush](https://selflystore.com/slush/)
- [FI] At Flow Festival (Helsinki, Suvilahti) the vendors are mostly independent restaurants: Street Gastro/Bun 2 Bun, Michelin-starred Grön, Way Bakery, Maannos (2019). Street Gastro also traded at Ruisrock and Sideways in 2019. — [Yrittäjät.fi, 7.8.2019](https://www.yrittajat.fi/uutiset/festivaali-on-ravintoloitsijalle-mahdollisuus-saada-nakyvyytta-ja-muokata-imagoa-keittion-on-oltava-nopeampi-kuin-kassa/)
- [FI] Flow Festival food policy (2024–2026): no red meat or poultry, all fish Finnish, and every restaurant offers at least one vegan dish. Valio (Oddlygood) was a festival partner in 2022 as a brand, not as a wholesaler. — [Flow Festival press materials 2022](https://www.flowfestival.com/current/uploads/2022/06/2022-Flow-Festival-tiedote-9-6-2022.pdf); [Flow 2024 food release](https://flowfestival.com/current/uploads/2024/06/Flow-Festival-Ruokatiedote-6-6-2024.docx.pdf)
- [FI] Ruisrock (Turku) has a "Festival market" where small businesses sell to festivalgoers (for example Restaurant Muru, and Reissumies with a sandwich van). — [Ruisrock partners](https://ruisrock.fi/en/partners/); [Restaurant Muru](https://ruisrock.fi/?p=9536)
- [FI] The Ruisrock festival area is produced by Vantaan Festivaalit Oy, which rents infrastructure and orders production services from a range of subcontractors (Turku city project plan 2025–2027). — [City of Turku document](https://ah.turku.fi/kh/2025/0811018l/Images/2402564.pdf)
- [US] Levy at Chase Field (Phoenix): the culinary crew grows from 35 to 70 for playoffs, plus 10 support chefs flown in. — [Levy blog, 20.11.2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)

### Inferences
- In Finland, the "large spike" customer is the arena/stadium operator (NoHo, Restel Food & Events, Compass/Fazer), not each event. These are a few multi-site groups that run their own central kitchens, so the agent would be serving a group purchasing function with event-by-event peaks.
- Festival vendors are many small independents. Each buys a few hundred to a few thousand portions' worth of ingredients for 2–3 days, and few have dedicated purchasing staff, so they would benefit most from an agent that turns "menu × expected portions" into an order.
- Contract caterers' event arms (Compass/Amica, Sodexo, ISS) are probably tied to framework contracts with their main wholesaler. This could not be verified for Finland in this research.

### Gaps
- No source was found on the current Finnish structure of Sodexo Live, Amica event services or ISS event catering. Compass's acquisition of Fazer Food Services (understood to have closed around 2020) was not verified with a source here.
- Film/TV set catering, pop-ups and ghost kitchens with viral demand: no Finnish or Nordic source was found in this pass.
- Tuska, Provinssi and Helsinki Ice Hall's standing catering operator: no source on food operators or supply.
- No current Slush caterer (2023–2026) was identified.

## 2. How notice and headcount behave (lead times, late changes, weather, sell-outs)

### Takeaway
Menus are fixed months ahead, but the actual quantity is only settled days, or for sports playoffs hours, before the event. Weather can wipe out whole product lines at outdoor events. Typical contracts lock a final headcount 7–14 days ahead and allow about ±5% up to 48–72 hours before. Sports playoffs compress the whole plan-order-execute cycle into about 10 days or less.

### Cited Findings
- [FI] Nokia Arena 2023 Worlds: restaurant offerings were ready "months before the first shot". Each event has its own forecast and budget, and so far surprises have been positive (for example, unexpected demand from international visitors at a midday Czech game in 2022). — [Kespro, 2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla)
- [FI] Flow Festival vendor Street Gastro (2019) gave a rough baseline of 500 portions for a three-day event. Its 2019 target was 3,000 portions with a goal of 4,000, a 6–8× spread between baseline and target. Maannos estimated 1,000–2,000 portions, a 2× uncertainty band. — [Yrittäjät.fi, 2019](https://www.yrittajat.fi/uutiset/festivaali-on-ravintoloitsijalle-mahdollisuus-saada-nakyvyytta-ja-muokata-imagoa-keittion-on-oltava-nopeampi-kuin-kassa/)
- [FI] Weather: at Flow (about 2017) storms cancelled concerts, and Street Gastro's ceviche tent earned nothing because customers wanted hot food in the cold. Grön's first Flow had storms and it switched to simpler food. Maannos added a "summer soup for cold weather" as a hedge. — [Yrittäjät.fi, 2019](https://www.yrittajat.fi/uutiset/festivaali-on-ravintoloitsijalle-mahdollisuus-saada-nakyvyytta-ja-muokata-imagoa-keittion-on-oltava-nopeampi-kuin-kassa/)
- [FI] Vendors aim to "minimise waste while keeping enough raw materials if demand surprises". — [Yrittäjät.fi, 2019](https://www.yrittajat.fi/uutiset/festivaali-on-ravintoloitsijalle-mahdollisuus-saada-nakyvyytta-ja-muokata-imagoa-keittion-on-oltava-nopeampi-kuin-kassa/)
- [US] Levy, 2023 World Series at Chase Field: the culinary team "planned, ordered, and executed in 10 days", working over 160 hours. "You usually have months to plan a season", but playoffs bring new menus every round at very short notice. — [Levy blog, 2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)
- [US] Levy, Houston (Shell Energy Stadium): purchasing has to prepare for the home team advancing while also planning for elimination. "Suppliers must be quick, nimble, and willing to alter inventory levels based on the outcome of each game." — [Levy blog, 2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)
- [US] Levy's analytics arm E15 predicts staffing from ticket sales, weather and opponent data (Dodger Stadium). Supply chain meets E15 to review the predicted trends and volumes for each game. — [TeamSense webinar](https://www.teamsense.com/webinars/optimizing-game-day-dodger-stadium); [Levy blog, 2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)
- [US/UK, catering contracts] The final headcount is typically due 7–14 days before a wedding/event, with ±5% adjustments allowed up to 72 hours before and then locked. — [InvoiceQuickly caterer guide](https://invoicequickly.com/blog/how-to-invoice-caterer); [Agiled catering contract template](https://agiled.app/contract-templates/catering)
- [US] Some venues require the final count 14 days prior. — [Wentworth (Opal Collection) policies](https://www.opalcollection.com/wentworth/restaurants/wedding-menus/menu/guidelines-and-policies)
- [Global, events glossary] The "catering guarantee" is typically due 48–72 hours before an event (more common for corporate events), and the client is charged for that number regardless of attendance. — [AM World Group glossary](https://amworldgroup.com/glossary/events/catering-guarantee)
- [US] Caterers or venues often over-set by about 5% of the headcount. — [WeddingWire forum, practitioner comments](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-forums/caterer-headcount-deadline/8fcd1bd7d2c9e968.html) (forum source, low reliability)
- [US] Weather: cold weather increases hot food and drink sales, and rain suppresses movement and reduces purchases (concessions forecasting guide, vendor content). — [FoodSight guide](https://foodsight-marketing.pages.dev/blog/stadium-event-catering-waste) (vendor marketing, treat cautiously)

### Inferences
- There are two order "moments". (1) A base order placed days ahead, driven by the guarantee or forecast. (2) Top-up orders within 24 hours, or during the event, driven by actual sales, weather and sell-outs. The second is where speed matters most and where Finnish buyers currently fall back on cash & carry (see section 4).
- A ±5% headcount change at 72 hours on a 2,000-cover gala is about 100 covers. That typically means changing a few line quantities rather than the menu, which suits automated re-calculation.
- Sports playoffs are a clear "order differs every time, needed fast" use case. In Finland the analogues are Liiga/ice hockey playoffs at Nokia Arena and Helsinki, and national-team football at the Olympic Stadium. There is no Finnish evidence of how these are ordered.

### Gaps
- No Finnish data on typical notice for weddings, corporate galas or pitopalvelu (party catering) orders, or on how often headcounts change late.
- No data on sell-out frequency or lost sales at Finnish festivals.

## 3. Order flow: who builds the order, how menus × covers become ingredients, systems, contracts vs spot

### Takeaway
Menus and product ranges are fixed with a primary wholesaler months ahead; NoHo + Kespro is the documented Finnish case. Per-event volumes come from a forecast or budget, and supply chain staff align with chefs on menu changes. Catering software (Total Party Planner, Apicbase and others) covers recipe costing, menu scaling and purchase-order management. However, no vendor documentation was found showing automatic conversion of event covers into wholesaler orders. That step appears to be manual or semi-manual.

### Cited Findings
- [FI] NoHo Partners (Nokia Arena) on Kespro: "a lot of groundwork has been done with Kespro". Kespro's sales manager says information flows seamlessly between the two companies, and the products to be sold had been known for some time. — [Kespro, 2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla)
- [FI] NoHo uses "generic product choices" to avoid waste, meaning products that can be reused across outlets. Leftovers go to the arena's lunch service. — [Kespro, 2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla)
- [FI] Restel Food & Events develops event products in its own kitchens (a production-kitchen model) rather than under pre-existing brands. — [Restel/STT, 2026](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/71882959/abreun-konsertit-saavat-oman-ravintolatarjontansa-veikkaus-arenalla?publisherId=55320854)
- [US] At Levy, culinary teams align with supply chain on menu changes so that they know what is coming and who to coordinate with. A dedicated purchasing team orders. — [Levy blog, 2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)
- [US] Centerplate (Levi's Stadium, about 2015) used "Canopy", a food inventory management tool, to adjust stand inventory on the fly. — [Sports Business Daily, 2015](https://www.sportsbusinessdaily.com/Journal/Issues/2015/02/23/In-Depth/Levis-Stadium.aspx)
- [Software] Total Party Planner: features include menu and recipe costing, inventory order tracking, beverage/equipment order tracking and an "Express Order Form". Plans start at $65/month (Nibble plan, 1 user). — [PerfectVenue review](https://www.perfectvenue.com/post/total-party-planner-review); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/catering-software/a/total-party-planner); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/total-party-planner-profile/)
- [Software, BE/EU] Apicbase: features include forecasting and planning, production planning, purchase order management and sales planning. It coordinates "procurement and production in your central production unit with local restaurant orders" and lists communal, system and commercial catering among its sectors. — [Softguide – Apicbase](https://softguide.com/program/apicbase); [GetApp – Apicbase](https://www.getapp.com/retail-consumer-services-software/a/apicbase-food-management)
- [US] Restaurant365 documents "shopping lists" for building purchase orders, an example of the order-guide pattern. — [Restaurant365 docs](https://docs.restaurant365.com/docs/purchase-orders-set-up-shopping-lists)
- [UK] The FSA notes that some SME caterers "may be reliant on local cash and carries or other smaller suppliers to source ingredients as and when they need them". — [FSA – PPDS guidance for event caterers](https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/prepacked-for-direct-sale-ppds-allergen-labelling-changes-for-event-caterers)
- [FI] Valio Aimo: ordering requires a customership and customer number. "VALO Tilauspalvelu" lets professionals order directly from major suppliers through Valio Aimo's nationwide logistics network (contract customers). — [Valio Aimo – VALO Tilauspalvelu](https://www.valioaimo.fi/en/become-a-customer/valo-tilauspalvelu-en/); [Valio Aimo – become a customer](https://www.valioaimo.fi/en/become-a-customer/)

### Inferences
- The documented Finnish pattern is one primary delivery wholesaler (Kespro for NoHo) plus that wholesaler's own cash & carry for top-ups. If this is typical, a buyer's preference for one partner is set at group level, and an agent would win by making the per-event order and the top-up faster and more accurate within that relationship.
- The step from menu × covers to ingredient list exists as recipe scaling in tools like Apicbase, Jamix and Nutritics. The step from ingredient list to a wholesaler order with correct pack sizes, substitutes and delivery slots is the gap an agent could fill. This is inference: no vendor documentation was found that confirms or rules out automatic per-event POs.

### Gaps
- No documentation was retrieved for Caterease, Planning Pod, Galley, Nutritics or Jamix purchasing workflows. Jamix is Finnish and widely used in public catering, so it is worth checking.
- No evidence on how many wholesalers Finnish event caterers use, or on Kespro vs Valio Aimo vs Metro share among event caterers.
- Who builds the order inside a Finnish event caterer (head chef vs purchasing manager) is not documented beyond NoHo's "operative food service manager" being the Kespro contact.

## 4. Logistics: cut-offs, same-day/express, temporary sites, cold chain, returns

### Takeaway
Arena operators combine central-warehouse deliveries, extra cold-storage containers and a nearby cash & carry for minute-level top-ups. NoHo/Kespro quote 18 minutes from the Nekala cash & carry dock to Nokia Arena. UK broadline cut-offs are about 5 pm for next-day delivery. "Emergency within 6 hours" exists in some public contracts but is not a standard published service. Unused stock is redeployed or donated, not returned.

### Cited Findings
- [FI] Nokia Arena 2023: Kespro's central warehouse logistics handle product deliveries, and cold storage containers provide extra capacity. Transport times are restricted during the championships (security/access windows). Quick supply comes from Kespro's Nekala cash & carry (noutotukku): "The roll cage waits at the loading dock, and in 18 minutes it's at Nokia Arena." — [Kespro, 2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla)
- [FI] Surplus from the 2023 Worlds was used in the arena's lunch service and donated to Ukraine House and parish food aid. — [Kespro, 2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla)
- [US] Levy playoffs: leftovers are shared with other local venues, donated, or kept as shelf-stable items. — [Levy blog, 2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)
- [UK] Brakes (Sysco): next-day delivery on orders placed before 5 pm, Monday–Saturday, with a £150 minimum for free delivery. Customers get an email the day before delivery listing any out-of-stocks so they can choose an alternative before cut-off. — [Brakes – London wholesale](https://www.brake.co.uk/wholesale-food-london); [Brakes – why Brakes](https://www.brake.co.uk/why-brakes)
- [UK, 2020] Bidfood's next-day cut-off was 4 pm (pandemic-era home-delivery scheme). — [Loughborough University news, 2020](https://www.lboro.ac.uk/internal/news/2020/april/catering-supplier-offers-home-deliveries)
- [UK, NHS contract, 2019] Bidfood must provide an emergency delivery within six hours "due to unforeseen circumstances or due to an event". — [NHS Wirral implementation pack](https://wuth.nhs.uk/media/13386/mtd-implementation-pack-final-051119.pdf)
- [UK] Freshways cash & carry markets 2-hour click & collect to caterers who "ran out of essentials". — [Freshways blog](https://freshwayscc.co.uk/blog/2-hour-wholesale-click-collect-caterer-guide/)
- [FI] Valio Aimo operates delivery wholesale (toimitustukku) with nationwide logistics. No public cut-off time was found in this research. — [Valio Aimo toimitustukku](https://www.valioaimo.fi/en/toimitustukku-en)

### Inferences
- In Finland, cash & carry (Kespro noutotukku, Metro) is currently the "express" channel for spike top-ups. The buyer, not the wholesaler, carries the logistics cost (staff time, vehicle). An agent that can trigger a same-day or click-and-collect pick at the nearest depot would replace a phone-and-drive process.
- Festival sites have restricted delivery windows and access passes, like the "restricted transport times" at Nokia Arena. Delivery-time accuracy (slot, gate, contact person) is therefore part of order accuracy for temporary sites.
- Returns of unused event stock appear rare. Operators redeploy or donate, which pushes them to order generic, reusable SKUs and to under-order and top up.

### Gaps
- Valio Aimo and Kespro order cut-offs, same-day options, minimum order values and delivery fees: not found publicly.
- Delivery to temporary festival sites in Finland (Suvilahti, Ruissalo), and the cold-chain arrangements there (reefer containers, ice), are undocumented beyond the Nokia Arena containers.

## 5. Exceptions: stock-outs, substitutions, allergens/special diets at scale, incidents

### Takeaway
Wholesalers handle stock-outs through pre-delivery out-of-stock notices with time to pick a substitute (Brakes). Allergen compliance at events is driven by regulation (EU FIC 1169/2011, UK Natasha's Law/PPDS) and depends on accurate product allergen data from suppliers, which UK trade bodies say is often poor. Finnish outbreak records list catering as an outbreak setting, but no specific large Finnish event-catering incident was found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- [UK] Brakes emails out-of-stocks the day before delivery so customers can select alternatives before cut-off. — [Brakes – why Brakes](https://www.brake.co.uk/why-brakes)
- [UK] Food that festival/event caterers pack before ordering (PPDS) must carry full ingredient labelling with allergens emphasised (Natasha's Law). Food ordered in advance is not PPDS, but allergen information must still be provided. — [FSA – PPDS guidance for event caterers](https://www.food.gov.uk/business-guidance/prepacked-for-direct-sale-ppds-allergen-labelling-changes-for-event-caterers); [NCASS/FSA webinar slides](https://www.food.gov.uk/sites/default/files/media/document/ncass-fsa-ppds-webinar-slides.pdf)
- [UK] Wholesalers reported poor allergen information from some suppliers during staff shortages and called for a central product allergen database. — [The Caterer – food supply chain allergen / Natasha's Law](https://thecaterer.com/all-content/food-supply-chain-allergen-natashas-law-restaurants)
- [UK] A venue checklist exists for managing dietary requirements at events (Meet Cambridge). — [Meet Cambridge checklist](https://assets.simpleviewinc.com/simpleview/image/upload/v1/clients/meetcambridge/Managing_Dietary_Requirements_at_Events_Checklist_for_VENUES_5a24ecda-de58-4277-a7c9-b2ce5305e8c6.pdf)
- [FI] Ruokavirasto's outbreak register (RYMY) lists catering (pitopalvelu) as a venue category for foodborne outbreaks (2000–2020). — [Ruokavirasto – outbreak venues since 2000](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/teemat/zoonoosikeskus/ruokamyrkytykset/tapahtumapaikat-vuodesta-2000.pdf)
- [FI] The largest foodborne outbreak in 2020–2022 was Salmonella Typhimurium in 2021 (728 ill). In 2016 more than 200 people fell ill with EHEC/EPEC. Neither is attributed to event catering in the snippet. — [Ruokavirasto publications 1/2024](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/globalassets/tietoa-meista/julkaisut/julkaisusarjat/julkaisuja/ruokaviraston_julkaisuja_1_2024.pdf); [MTV Uutiset](https://www.mtvuutiset.fi/makuja/artikkeli/ruokavirasto-runsaat-600-ihmista-sairastui-viime-vuonna-myrkytykseen-suomessa/8733846)
- [FI] Helsinki recorded 105 oyster-related food poisonings (city news; date not confirmed in snippet). — [City of Helsinki](https://www.hel.fi/fi/uutiset/helsingissa-on-tiedossa-105-osterien-aiheuttamaa-ruokamyrkytysta)
- [FI] Flow Festival requires every restaurant to offer at least one vegan dish and bans red meat and poultry. Sideways at Helsinki Ice Hall had an entirely plant-based menu. Diet constraints are set by the event organiser, not only by guests. — [Flow Festival news](https://www.flowfestival.com/en/news/read-the-latest/flow-festivals-food-program-merges-top-restaurants-with-a-sustainable-food-selection); [Restel/STT, 2026](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/71882959/abreun-konsertit-saavat-oman-ravintolatarjontansa-veikkaus-arenalla?publisherId=55320854)

### Inferences
- A substitution on a spike order is riskier than on a routine order. A substitute SKU can carry a different allergen profile, and the caterer may already have printed labels or menu cards for thousands of items. An agent should treat any substitution as needing an allergen/spec diff and explicit approval.
- Organiser-level rules (no meat, Finnish fish only, vegan option required) act as hard constraints on what an agent may substitute. These are distinct from guest-level special diets.

### Gaps
- No Finnish/Nordic incident found of a stock-out or wrong delivery wrecking a large event's catering.
- No data on how Finnish event caterers produce labelled special-diet meals at scale (for example for Slush or corporate galas), or on the share of special-diet covers.

## 6. Price sensitivity: do speed and reliability trump unit price?

### Takeaway
The evidence that reliability ranks above price is mostly UK survey data, plus Finnish procurement criteria that weight responsiveness and quality alongside price/quality ratio. Direct evidence that event caterers pay express fees is thin: only vendor examples of emergency charges were found, no measured market rates. The cost of failure is a lost sales window that cannot be recovered (a stand that runs out on match night), which supports the hypothesis without proving it.

### Cited Findings
- [UK] In a CGA/Prestige survey reported by The Caterer, operators ranked reliability as the top requirement of a supplier, ahead of quality and range. Nine in ten operators reported Covid-era supply disruption. — [The Caterer – CGA Prestige survey](https://thecaterer.com/all-content/nine-ten-operators-covid-supply-disruption-cga-prestige-survey)
- [UK] Brita research: 51% of operators found suppliers had become slightly less reliable over five years. Education caterers rank product quality above reliability, and supplier reputation lowest. — [Public Sector Catering – Brita research](https://publicsectorcatering.co.uk/in-depth/brita-professional-releases-education-catering-research)
- [UK] Driver shortages cut inbound deliveries to foodservice suppliers to about 70–80% fulfilment (normally 98–100%), and caterers were advised to hold emergency stock. — [The Caterer – overcoming supply chain challenges](https://www.thecaterer.com/business/overcoming-supply-chainc-hallenges)
- [UK, vendor example] A cash & carry blog cites emergency delivery charges of £15–£25 on a £26 order, an illustration rather than market data. — [Freshways blog](https://freshwayscc.co.uk/blog/2-hour-wholesale-click-collect-caterer-guide/)
- [FI] The Helsinki Olympic Stadium catering tender weighted operating concept, resources, quality management, responsiveness and innovation alongside price/quality ratio. — [Fazer press release, 2019](https://news.cision.com/fazer-group/r/fazer-food-services-selected-as-the-food-service-partner-of-the-helsinki-olympic-stadium,c2742454)
- [US] Levy says suppliers "must be quick, nimble, and willing to alter inventory levels based on the outcome of each game", treating flexibility as a selection criterion. — [Levy blog, 2023](https://www.levyrestaurants.com/blog/2023-11-20-creating-playoff-magic-across-the-country)
- [FI] Festival restaurateurs describe events as "risky and demanding". Street Gastro reduced its number of events to focus on those expected to succeed, and the kitchen "must be faster than the cash register". — [Yrittäjät.fi, 2019](https://www.yrittajat.fi/uutiset/festivaali-on-ravintoloitsijalle-mahdollisuus-saada-nakyvyytta-ja-muokata-imagoa-keittion-on-oltava-nopeampi-kuin-kassa/)
- [US] West Monroe surveyed restaurant operators on what drives distributor satisfaction (price and reliability among the criteria). The weighting was not retrieved. — [West Monroe white paper](https://www.westmonroepartners.com/Insights/White-Papers/Setting-Foodserivce-Distributors-Apart)

### Inferences
- For arena and festival concessions, revenue is concentrated in a few hours: 12,000 spectators, about 2,000 VIP covers per game, and 10,000 wings a day at one Nokia Arena outlet. A stock-out of a hero item costs far more than any plausible unit-price saving. The documented 18-minute cash & carry top-up shows buyers already pay in staff time and logistics for speed.
- For corporate galas and weddings, the failure cost is reputational and contractual: the client is billed on the guarantee and expects the full menu. Exact quantity and allergen/spec accuracy are therefore likely to matter more than price at order time. Price is negotiated earlier, at menu/contract stage.
- Festival vendors may be more price-sensitive than arena operators because their margins on a few-day event are thin and risky. Their highest pain is quantity uncertainty (2–8× bands), so flexible top-up may matter more to them than express base delivery.

### Gaps
- No Finnish data on express delivery fees, event caterers' gross margins, or penalties in event catering contracts.
- No survey specific to event caterers ranking speed/accuracy vs price.

## 7. Finland/Nordics: who supplies the big events and how they order

### Takeaway
The one well-documented Finnish case is NoHo Partners + Kespro at Nokia Arena (2023 Worlds). It combines groundwork months ahead, per-event forecast and budget, central-warehouse deliveries in restricted windows, cold-storage containers, and an 18-minute cash & carry top-up. Kespro (Kesko) appears as the visible wholesale partner in Finnish event coverage. Valio Aimo appeared in this research only through Valio as a brand partner (Flow 2022, Oddlygood), not as an event wholesaler.

### Cited Findings
- [FI] NoHo Partners runs Nokia Arena catering and is supplied by Kespro (central warehouse plus Nekala cash & carry). — [Kespro, 2023](https://www.kespro.com/ideat-ja-inspiraatiot/artikkelit/mm-kiekko-kansa-syo-ja-juo-hyvin-nokia-arenalla); [Nokia Arena](https://nokiaarena.fi/en/organize-an-event/)
- [FI] Kespro publishes content on supporting restaurants at other events, for example supplying ingredients to restaurants at a food fair (Ruokamessut). — [Kespro – Ruokamessujen ravintolatarjonta](https://www.kespro.com/sv/ideer-och-inspiration/artiklar/ruokamessujen-ravintolatarjonta-on-aistien-juhlaa)
- [FI] Restel Food & Events runs Veikkaus Arena F&B (head chef Riku Ahonen, F&B manager Oliver Hurskainen) and does event-specific catering at Helsingin Jäähalli (Sideways). — [Restel/STT, 2026](https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/71882959/abreun-konsertit-saavat-oman-ravintolatarjontansa-veikkaus-arenalla?publisherId=55320854)
- [FI] Olympic Stadium: Fazer Food Services was selected in 2019 through public procurement, and Compass Group FI now operates Bistro Stadion there. — [Fazer, 2019](https://news.cision.com/fazer-group/r/fazer-food-services-selected-as-the-food-service-partner-of-the-helsinki-olympic-stadium,c2742454); [Compass Group FI](https://www.compass-group.fi/ravintolat-ja-ruokalistat/muut-avoimet-ravintolat/kaupungit/helsinki/olympiastadion/bistro-stadion/)
- [FI] Slush: Fazer Food Services catered 2017 with about 100 staff for about 10,000 visitors a day. Attendance was about 12,000 in 2022. — [Fazer/Cision 2017](https://mb.cision.com/Main/964/2403901/760447.pdf); [Selfly Store](https://selflystore.com/slush/)
- [FI] Flow Festival (Helsinki, 14–16 Aug 2026) vendors are independent restaurants operating under organiser food rules. Valio Oddlygood was a 2022 partner. — [Flow 2026 press release](https://www.flowfestival.com/uploads/2026/06/Flow-Festival-tiedote-5-6-2026.pdf); [Flow 2022 press release](https://www.flowfestival.com/current/uploads/2022/06/2022-Flow-Festival-tiedote-9-6-2022.pdf)
- [FI] Messukeskus (Helsinki Expo) offers in-house event services, including catering, for events held there (Slush venue). — [Messukeskus event services](https://messukeskus.com/event-services/?lang=en)

### Inferences
- Kespro appears to own the "event wholesaler" narrative in Finnish trade content: a published case study, and cash & carry depots near arenas. For Valio Aimo, an agent that matches or beats the 18-minute cash & carry top-up with a guaranteed fast-delivery or pick-up order, and that handles the large pre-event order accurately, would compete directly on that ground. This is based on one case study; Valio Aimo's own event customers were not found publicly.
- Likely Finnish target segments for the agent: (a) arena/stadium operators (NoHo, Restel Food & Events, Compass at the Olympic Stadium) during tournaments, playoffs and concert series; (b) contract caterers' event teams for one-off large events (Slush-type conferences at Messukeskus, Compass/Amica); (c) festival restaurant vendors (Flow, Ruisrock, Sideways, Tuska), who have high quantity uncertainty and little purchasing capacity; (d) pitopalvelu/wedding caterers, who have guarantee-driven orders. This is a synthesis, not a sourced ranking.

### Gaps
- No public information found on which wholesaler supplies Flow, Ruisrock, Tuska or Provinssi vendors, or Slush's current caterer.
- No Finnish trade-press interviews (Restauranttivisio, Kehittyvä Ruokapalvelu, MaRa) on event purchasing were retrieved in this pass. These are the obvious next sources.
- No Nordic (Sweden/Norway/Denmark) evidence was gathered, for example Way Out West, Roskilde, or Sodexo/Compass Live operations at Friends Arena/Strawberry Arena.
- Valio Aimo's order cut-off, express options and event-customer case studies: not publicly found.
